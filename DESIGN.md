# ARUN.S Portfolio Design System

## Direction

An editorial portfolio that feels like a considered print publication translated into a responsive product. The page uses an ink-blue and warm-paper palette, oversized typographic moments, and restrained motion to make the work feel authored rather than templated.

## Content hierarchy

- Introduction: who Arun is and what he builds.
- About: personal context and working point of view.
- Skills: capability areas and technical stack.
- Experience: past roles and progression.
- Projects: three clearly labelled fictional end-to-end concepts.
- Process: how work moves from framing to build.
- Contact: a functional inquiry path.

## Tokens

- Paper: `#e6dbc7`
- Paper shadow: `#d9ccb3`
- Ink: `#062b8a`
- Ink secondary: `#1747b9`
- Muted line: `#9aa6c3`
- Display type: TikTok Sans
- Interaction: 200–800ms transform/opacity transitions, with reduced-motion fallback.

## Signature details

- Newspaper-style issue metadata and figure captions.
- Oversized editorial wordmark with a compact interactive GLB figure.
- Chapter-like experience cards that let visitors move through past work.
- Project case-study overlays for the mock products.

## Responsive behavior

The desktop layout uses asymmetric editorial composition and the mobile layout collapses to a single readable column. Navigation becomes compact, project imagery moves beneath copy, and the GLB figure scales down to preserve the ARUN lettering.

## Accessibility and states

Interactive project rows are keyboard accessible. Contact submission exposes loading, error, and success states. Reduced motion disables reveal and parallax animation. Images and the 3D scene have descriptive labels.
